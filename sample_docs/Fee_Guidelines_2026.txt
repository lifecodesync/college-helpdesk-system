================================================================================
NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
TUITION FEE PAYMENT, SCHOLARSHIPS & REFUND POLICIES (2026-2027)
================================================================================

SECTION 1: PAYMENT SCHEDULE & MODES
1.1 Semester tuition fees must be remitted strictly by the 15th of the opening month of the semester.
1.2 Approved Payment Methods:
    - Online ERP Student Portal using Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, or UPI (Zero surcharge).
    - NEFT / RTGS to College Bank Account (details available on student dashboard).
    - Cash transactions are strictly prohibited at the Accounts counter.
1.3 Late Fine Provisions:
    - Days 1 to 7 after due date: Grace period with Rs. 100 late fine per day.
    - Days 8 to 21 after due date: Surcharge of Rs. 2,500 + Rs. 150 per additional day.
    - After 30 days: Provisional registration canceled; student must seek Director approval for reinstatement.

SECTION 2: MERIT SCHOLARSHIPS & FEE WAIVERS
2.1 Institutional Merit Scholarship:
    - Top 3 rank holders per branch in each semester receive 25%, 15%, and 10% tuition fee waiver respectively.
    - Eligible students must maintain a CGPA above 8.5 with no history of backlogs or disciplinary sanctions.
2.2 Educational Bank Loans:
    - Bonafide certificate and annual expenditure estimation letters can be obtained within 48 hours of ticket submission via Helpdesk.

SECTION 3: REFUND POLICY ON WITHDRAWAL
Time of Withdrawal Notice                          | Refund of Tuition Fee
15 days or more before semester commencement date   | 100% (minus Rs. 1,000 processing fee)
Less than 15 days before commencement date         | 90%
Within 15 days after commencement date              | 80%
Between 16 to 30 days after commencement date      | 50%
Beyond 30 days after commencement date             | 0% (Only Caution Deposit refunded)
Caution money deposits are 100% refundable after clearance from Library, Laboratories, Hostel, and Sports Complex.
