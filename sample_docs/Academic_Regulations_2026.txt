NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
OFFICIAL ACADEMIC REGULATIONS & DEGREE REQUIREMENTS (2026-2027)
================================================================================

SECTION 1: PROGRAMME STRUCTURE & CREDITS
1.1 The Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree program is structured over 8 semesters (4 academic years).
1.2 To qualify for the degree, a student must earn a minimum of 160 academic credits comprising:
    - Basic Science Courses: 24 credits
    - Engineering Science Courses: 20 credits
    - Humanities and Social Sciences: 12 credits
    - Professional Core Courses: 58 credits
    - Professional Electives: 18 credits
    - Open Electives: 12 credits
    - Project Work, Internship & Seminar: 16 credits

SECTION 2: ATTENDANCE NORMS & CONDONATION
2.1 Minimum Attendance: Every candidate must secure a minimum aggregate attendance of 75% in theory and practical sessions across all registered subjects.
2.2 Condonation of Shortage:
    - Attendance between 65.0% and 74.9% may be condoned by the Academic Dean only on medical grounds (with hospital discharge certificate) or official participation in university-approved sports/cultural events.
    - Prescribed condonation fee is Rs. 1,000 per course subject.
2.3 Detainment:
    - Students having attendance strictly below 65% in any course will NOT be eligible for condonation and shall be detained. Detained students must re-register for the course in subsequent odd/even semesters.

SECTION 3: CONTINUOUS INTERNAL EVALUATION (CIE) & SEMESTER EXAMS (ESE)
3.1 Course evaluation ratio: Continuous Internal Assessment (40 Marks) + End Semester Examination (60 Marks) = 100 Marks.
3.2 Internal Assessment Breakdown:
    - Two mid-term tests: 20 Marks (Best of two or average as declared by department)
    - Assignment / Quiz / Mini Project: 10 Marks
    - Attendance & Class Participation: 10 Marks
3.3 Minimum Passing Criteria: A candidate must secure at least 40% in End Semester Exam (24/60) and 45% aggregate in total marks (45/100) to pass the subject (Grade C).
